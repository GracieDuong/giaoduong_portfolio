View Data Assumptions & Limitations

The dataset contains historical consumer loan applications, borrower information, loan status, funded amount, total payments received, interest rate, DTI, grade, term, and loan purpose.

The dataset does not include repayment schedules, Days Past Due (DPD), collection actions, or monthly loan performance snapshots. Therefore, true DPD monitoring and vintage curve analysis cannot be calculated directly.

This report uses loan status, bad loan rate, repayment indicator, issue-month risk trend, and segmentation analysis as proxy indicators for portfolio risk monitoring.
