Data & Assumptions

The dataset contains historical consumer loan applications, borrower information, loan status, funded amount, total payments received, interest rate, DTI, grade, term, and loan purpose.

The dataset does not include repayment schedules, Days Past Due (DPD), collection actions, or monthly loan performance snapshots. Therefore, true DPD monitoring and vintage curve analysis cannot be calculated directly.

This report uses the following proxy indicators for portfolio risk monitoring:

  • Loan Status
  • Bad Loan Rate
  • Repayment Indicator
  • Issue-Month Risk Trend
  • Risk Segmentation by Grade, Term, DTI, Rate Band, and Purpose

Section 03 · Monthly Risk Monitoring

Monthly risk movement shows portfolio quality volatility

Bad loan rate fluctuated across origination months, with higher-risk months appearing around May and December. Because the dataset does not include DPD or vintage snapshots, this trend should be treated as an issue-month risk proxy.

Section 04 · Recommendations

Risk management recommendations

The analysis suggests four priority actions to improve portfolio monitoring, policy control, and early risk detection.

Recommended Risk Control Actions
Prioritized based on bad loan rate concentration and funded amount exposure.
Finding Priority Risk Impact Recommended Action Monitoring Metric
60-month loans have a 22.34% bad loan rate High Long-term exposure with higher default risk Apply tighter approval conditions and monitor 60-month portfolio separately Bad rate by term
Grade F and G loans exceed 30% bad loan rate High High concentration of credit loss risk Limit exposure and introduce stricter credit policy for Grade E-G Bad rate by grade
Small business loans show the highest bad loan rate Medium Segment-level risk concentration Create a separate SME review pipeline and require stronger income verification Bad rate by purpose
High DTI borrowers show elevated risk Medium Lower repayment capacity under financial stress Introduce DTI trigger rules and automated early-warning alerts Bad rate by DTI band
Final insight: The portfolio shows healthy growth and strong repayment activity, but credit risk is concentrated in specific borrower and loan segments. The key business action is not to reduce growth, but to apply more granular risk controls across grade, term, purpose, and DTI-based segments.
