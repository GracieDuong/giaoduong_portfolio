Section 01 · Business Context

Strong revenue growth masking structural problems

O&F operates across North Asia, Oceania, and Southeast Asia. Revenue was growing — but deeper analysis revealed operational inefficiencies affecting profitability, customer experience, and retention.

System view

Customer → Purchase → Discount → Delivery → Profit → Retention
Analytical approach: This project treated the business as a connected system rather than analyzing metrics in isolation — tracing how discount decisions in one part of the funnel created downstream effects on profitability and customer retention.
Dashboard overview
Section 02 · Key Findings

Three structural problems identified

Each finding connects to a different layer of the business system — pricing strategy, customer intelligence, and operational execution.

Finding 01

Revenue growth was eroding profitability

  • Products discounted above 40% frequently generated negative profit
  • Some transactions recorded losses up to −$2.7K per order
  • Revenue growth relied heavily on discount-driven volume, not margin
Business impact: The business was optimizing for top-line revenue growth rather than sustainable per-unit profitability — a pattern that compounds over time as discount expectations set in.

Finding 02

Customer segmentation was underutilized

  • "Others" segment generated the highest revenue at ~$1.12M
  • High-value "Champion" customers were not given differentiated treatment
  • RFM segments were not integrated into marketing or pricing decisions
Business impact: High-value customers were treated similarly to lower-value segments, limiting retention and upsell opportunities at the top of the customer pyramid.

Finding 03

Delivery performance was weakening customer experience

  • Late order rates reached 35–45% across regions
  • Southeast Asia experienced the highest delivery delays
  • Standard shipping consistently underperformed vs other tiers
Business impact: Operational efficiency was prioritized over customer experience, increasing churn risk especially among high-value customer segments that expect reliable fulfillment.
Section 03 · Recommendations

Four priority actions

Actions mapped to each structural problem identified — prioritized by potential impact on profitability and retention.

Pricing

Introduce discount thresholds at the 40% level. Track profit-per-order alongside revenue to make margin erosion visible in reporting. Reward high-margin products with lower discount exposure.

Customer Strategy

Prioritize loyalty and upsell campaigns for Champion and high-value segments. Differentiate communication, offers, and service levels by RFM tier — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Operations

Introduce priority shipping lanes for high-value customers in Southeast Asia. Review standard shipping SLA performance and investigate root causes of delivery delays by region.

Retention

Develop lifecycle-based engagement campaigns. Create re-engagement flows for lapsed Champions and at-risk segments before they fully churn out of the active customer pool.

Section 04 · Final Insight

The problem was not weak sales — it was an unoptimized system

O&F was growing. Revenue was up, markets were expanding, and customer acquisition was working. The core issue was not at the top of the funnel.

The business was optimizing for revenue volume rather than sustainable profitability and customer value. Discount decisions were eroding margins. Fulfillment failures were eroding experience. Segmentation data existed but was not being used to drive differentiated treatment.

This project focused on identifying those operational trade-offs, tracing root causes across the customer-to-profit system, and building recommendations that target each layer separately — so that growth and quality can move together.
